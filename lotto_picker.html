Numbers, as the mathematics gives them (962 official-verified draws, per-game fitting T = 192/193/192/193/192; corrected per two gpt-6-astra reviews): conditional on M1(100), the maximum-predictive six-set of each game carries R = 1.48–1.64 on P(jackpot) versus a uniform pick, with 95% credible intervals whose lower ends are 0.98–1.07. Technical appendix

Data (official, pcso.gov.ph SearchLottoResult, fetched 2026-09-06): 984 draws Jun 1 2025 – Sep 5 2026 with jackpot and winning-bet counts; the 962-row canonical file (Jun 11 2025 – Sep 5 2026) matches the official page on every row (834/834 prior rows re-verified exactly; 128 new rows official_verified, cross-checked 128/128 on lottopcso.com and 100/100 on pcsodraw.com, whose 20-draw window leaves 28 draws covered by draw-number continuity only). Provenance: datasets/pcso-lotto/provenance/pcso_refresh_2026-09-06.json + raw HTML. Prize matrix, ticket price and 9 PM draw time: official PCSO game pages fetched 2026-09-06 (provenance/raw_2026-09-06/gamepage_*.html.gz).

Next-draw posterior (src/pcso_next_draw_posterior.py → results/pcso_next_draw_posterior_2026-09-06.json): likelihood ∏i wici / e6(w)T (the e6 normalizer is what a multinomial-on-counts model omits, and Dirichlet conjugacy does not survive it); posterior expectations by importance sampling from Dirichlet(a+c) with weights z(w)−T, z(w) = e6(n·w)/C(n,6), 200,000 samples per game (effective size ≥ 195,000). BF10(a) = n6T Γ(na)/Γ(na+6T) ∏i Γ(a+ci)/Γ(a) · EDir(a+c)[z(w)−T]. The conditional probability that some ball's inclusion deviates more than 10% from 6/n is ≈1 under M1(100) in every game — a property of the conditional model, not evidence for it; at equal model odds it is 0.51–0.82; under a=1000 the independent reconstruction reports 0.012–0.029. Static-weight inference: it does not estimate a changing "hotness" or Markov mechanism. Independent reconstruction by Codex gpt-6-astra (results/codex_review_2026-09-06.md) uses the same model and reaches the same conclusions; its point estimates differ at the third decimal and its order-1 test uses likelihood-ratio ordering, whereas this page's overlap test is an exact absolute-deviation test on the total overlap of consecutive draws. The 6/42 sixth place is tied: 18 or 35 can replace 6 with the same predictive probability.

Random low-popularity pair: a uniform 12-number sample by Fisher–Yates with the browser's cryptographic RNG (rejection sampling removes modulo bias), split into two disjoint tickets; the pair is accepted when both tickets' conscious-selection index is at or below the estimated 40th-percentile cutoff of the uniform CSI distribution for that pool (full-precision cutoffs from results/pcso_strategy_backtest_2026-09-06.json: 6/42 0.024313725 · 6/45 0.009090909 · 6/49 0.028184282 · 6/55 0.017210952 · 6/58 0.003333333; CSI is discrete, so the accepted fraction is not exactly 40%). If no pair is accepted within 20,000 proposals the page says so instead of showing rejected tickets. CSI ingredients: excess share of numbers ≤31, ≤12 and {1,3,5,7,9,11,12,13}; flags for arithmetic progressions, runs, common divisors, single last digit, single parity; weights are literature-motivated declared constants (Cook–Clotfelter, Stern–Cover, Simon, Farrell et al., Baker–McHale, Roger–Broihanne, Polin et al. motivate the ingredients; the decimal weights are the lab's declaration, not estimates from those papers). Same function as src/csi_popularity.py (parity self-test below). PCSO validation (984 draws, CSI standardized within game, winners permuted within game): T1 = +0.74 sd (raw p 5e-05), T2 = +0.16 rank correlation (raw p 1e-04), Holm p = 1e-04 both; Poisson pseudo-MLE with game effects and HC1 sandwich: winning bets ∝ exp(0.52·zCSI) conditional on game and jackpot, count ratio per SD 1.68 [1.44, 1.95]. Ticket sales are not published, so this is a count contrast at equal game and jackpot, not popularity per purchased bet. The zCSI shown per ticket uses the fitted within-game mean/SD of the observed draws (the covariate the slope refers to). G0 exploratory.

Backtest (results/pcso_strategy_backtest_2026-09-06.json, 834 draws after a 30-draw warm-up, 2 tickets per play, 300 replicates per draw for uniform/v1/v2 and 100 for the other rules; conditional Monte Carlo null with the generated tickets held fixed and the draws regenerated, m=9,999; Holm over 7 rules): Dt = ∑i at,i Xt,i with a = qs − q0; Var0(Dt) = 6(n−6)/(n(n−1)) ∑i at,i2; Z = ∑Dt / √∑Var0.

EV model (uniform-draw scenario M0, all tickets and tiers on the same model): hypergeometric tiers hk = C(6,k)C(n−6,6−k)/C(n,6); official Feb-2026 matrix (ticket ₱25; Category II/III fixed pools shared per winning bet, Category IV fixed); co-winner count K ~ Poisson(N·hk) under an assumed uniform, independent competitor-bet model (the results estimate no lower-tier co-winner distribution); the 20% tax is applied to each realized individual share above ₱10,000 inside the expectation, E[T(A/(1+K))]. The posterior jackpot-probability multipliers Rmix(A), Rmix(B) are shown beside the tickets as separate information and are not folded into the EV.
