What this page changes: only how much you would keep if you won (two disjoint tickets; low-popularity combinations). What it cannot change: the chance of winning. On 984 official draws (Jun 2025–Sep 2026), draws whose winning combination scored in the top third of the popularity index were shared 2.8× as often as the bottom third (kb card 28, G0 exploratory); no selection rule — hot, cold, overdue, repeat, Markov, or these filters — changed the match rate against the actual draws (draw-paired backtest, 1.67M tickets).

Algorithm v2 (replaces the four pattern filters): tickets are drawn uniformly with the browser's cryptographic RNG as one disjoint 12-number sample; each ticket's conscious-selection index (CSI: excess share of numbers ≤31, ≤12 and lucky digits {1,3,5,7,9,11,12,13}; flags for arithmetic progressions, runs of consecutive numbers, common divisors, single last digit, single parity — weights declared from Cook–Clotfelter, Stern–Cover, Simon, Farrell et al., Baker–McHale, Roger–Broihanne, Polin et al.) must fall in the bottom 40% of the uniform CSI distribution for that pool (q40: 6/42 0.0243 · 6/45 0.0091 · 6/49 0.0282 · 6/55 0.0172 · 6/58 0.0033). The old rules (all ≤31, 4+ consecutive, full arithmetic progression, one last digit) are a strict subset. Identical function to src/csi_popularity.py (parity self-test below). Validation on PCSO data: within-game standardized CSI of winner draws is +0.74 sd above winner-less draws (stratified permutation p = 5e-05, floor), rank correlation +0.16 (p = 1e-04); Poisson GLM with game effects: winning bets ∝ exp(0.52·zCSI). Grade G0 exploratory: weights fixed before the run, one year of data, winners are winning bets (one bettor can hold several), only the jackpot tier is observable. Nothing here is a prediction of the next numbers.

Backtest (results/pcso_strategy_backtest_2026-09-06.json, 834 draws × 2 tickets, replicates paired by draw, sign-flip null, Šidák α=0.0073 for 7 rules): mean matches per ticket vs uniform on the same draws — v1 filters +0.0040 (p=0.0061), v2 CSI +0.0081 (p=0.014), hot +0.012 (p=0.46), cold −0.041 (p=0.010), overdue −0.001 (p=0.95), repeat-last −0.004 (p=0.77), Markov +0.007 (p=0.67).
